# LogiCora — Master Product Roadmap & AI Execution Brief

Version: 2026-06-07  
Purpose: Master context file for Codex, Claude, V0, and future AI coding/design assistants.

---

## 0. One-line definition

**LogiCora is a lifelong Learning Operating System: a national-scale education passport, AI mentor, verified portfolio, course marketplace, institution CRM, and gamified learning ecosystem that follows a learner from age 3 through adulthood and employment.**

LogiCora is not only a course website, not only an LMS, and not only a quiz app.

It must support two paths:

1. **Startup/Public Mode** — students, parents, teachers, course centers, institutions, and employers can use it without ministry approval.
2. **Government/Ministry Mode** — optional national layer for schools, regions, ministry analytics, and transparent education records.

The platform must survive and grow even if the ministry does not adopt it.

---

## 1. Core Vision

LogiCora stores and analyzes a person's full learning journey:

- Preschool learning
- School progress
- Attendance
- Quizzes
- Courses
- Certificates
- Competitions
- Projects
- Hobbies
- Interests
- Skills
- Teacher recommendations
- Career signals
- Portfolio achievements
- Employment-ready verified profile

Long-term goal:

> Replace traditional CVs with a verified lifelong education profile.

Instead of sending `CV.pdf`, a user shares:

```text
logicora.az/portfolio/username
```

That profile shows verified achievements, certificates, projects, skill graph, learning timeline, and career readiness.

---

## 2. Product Philosophy

Every child should have a digital education passport starting from early childhood.

The product must support both:

1. **Full lifelong profile** — age 3 to adulthood.
2. **Late-entry profile** — someone can join at age 16, 25, or 30+ and still build a useful verified learning/career profile.

## 4. Core System Modules

These modules are part of the core product and should exist even without ministry adoption.

### 4.1 Education Passport

A lifelong profile containing:

- Personal learning history
- Certificates
- Achievements
- Competitions
- Courses
- School records
- University records
- Projects
- Hobbies
- Skills
- Recommendations
- Verified accomplishments

The education passport is the heart of LogiCora.

---

### 4.2 Learning Timeline

A chronological timeline from age 3 to adulthood.

Example:

```text
Age 5 — learned fruits, colors, animals
Age 8 — showed interest in logic games
Age 11 — math performance increased
Age 15 — completed Python course
Age 18 — joined AI competition
Age 25 — uses portfolio for employment
```

---

### 4.3 Interest Timeline

Interests should be tracked over years, not decided instantly.

Example:

```text
Age 5: animals, colors, drawing
Age 9: puzzles, reading
Age 13: robotics, math
Age 16: programming, AI
Age 21: data science, startup building
```

The system must understand that interests change over time.

---

### 4.4 Skill Graph

A visual and analytical skill map.

Skill categories:

- Math
- Logic
- Reading
- Writing
- Programming
- Creativity
- Leadership
- Communication
- Teamwork
- Problem solving
- Discipline
- Technical curiosity
- Scientific thinking

Skill Graph should grow over years.

---

### 4.5 Career Mentor AI

The AI must never decide the user's future.

Bad:

```text
You must become a programmer.
```

Good:

```text
Based on your long-term learning patterns, these fields may fit you well:
- Software Engineering
- Data Analysis
- Robotics
```

The final choice must always belong to the person.

---

### 4.6 Logi & Cora AI Companion System

Logi and Cora are not childish mascots.

They are premium AI personas.

#### Logi

- Color: blue
- Personality: analytical, logical, structured
- Focus: math, planning, performance, strategy, progress analysis

#### Cora

- Color: purple
- Personality: creative, motivational, communication-focused
- Focus: creativity, projects, confidence, communication, learning motivation

They are two personalities of the same AI intelligence.

They must act as:

- Learning companions
- Personal education historians
- Progress explainers
- Career mentors
- Memory-based guides

They should not behave like random chatbots.

They should understand the user's long-term learning history.

---

### 4.7 Logi/Cora Memory System

The AI should use long-term educational memory.

Example at age 25:

```text
You first showed interest in technology at age 11. Over the last 14 years, 72% of your learning activities were related to logic, math, programming, or technical projects.
```

Memory should include:

- Key milestones
- Learning improvements
- Repeated interests
- Skill growth
- Achievements
- Certificates
- Competitions
- Projects
- Behavioral learning patterns

---

### 4.8 Growth Story

AI-generated narrative of development.

Example:

```text
In 2020, your math results were unstable.
In 2021, you started improving.
In 2023, you joined your first competition.
In 2025, you completed your first programming course.
```

---

### 4.9 Memory Moments

Small emotional reminders.

Example:

```text
Today marks 5 years since your first certificate.
```

This creates long-term emotional connection with the platform.

---

### 4.10 Courses Marketplace

Courses must be part of the core system.

If ministry adoption does not happen, private course centers still need a platform to promote themselves, manage students, sell courses, and issue verified certificates.

Marketplace supports:

- Course discovery
- Teacher profiles
- Institution profiles
- Course search
- Course ratings
- Course enrollment
- Certificates
- Parent progress view
- Student portfolio integration

---

### 4.11 Institution CRM

This is critical.

Private education centers should not need to build their own CRM websites.

LogiCora should offer:

- Student management
- Teacher management
- Group management
- Lesson schedules
- Attendance
- Quizzes
- Exams
- Assignments
- Parent reports
- Payment tracking
- Certificate issuing
- Course performance analytics
- Student progress dashboards

This can become a major revenue source.

---

### 4.12 Verified Certificates

Certificates should be issued by institutions when possible.

Certificate levels:

1. **Institution Verified** — issued by verified course center, university, school, or organization.
2. **School Verified** — issued by a school.
3. **Ministry Verified** — issued or validated through government layer.
4. **User Uploaded** — user uploads image/PDF; appears as `Unverified` until reviewed.

Students should not be able to fake verified achievements.

---

### 4.13 Recommendation Letters

Teachers, mentors, institutions, and employers can add recommendations.

Examples:

```text
This student was active in the robotics club.
```

```text
Completed internship successfully.
```

This becomes part of the verified portfolio.

---

### 4.14 Employer Network

Employer role is required because LogiCora aims to replace traditional CVs.

Employer can:

- View public portfolio
- Request deeper access
- See verified certificates
- See projects
- See skill graph
- See recommendations
- Post internships/jobs in the future

Access must be permission-based.

---

### 4.15 Gamification Engine

Gamification is not the main product, but it drives engagement.

It includes:

- XP
- Levels
- Gems
- Hearts
- Streak
- Leagues
- Clans
- Daily Quests
- Weekly Mystery
- Knowledge Arena
- Elo rating

Gamification must feel premium, not childish.

---

### 4.16 Knowledge Arena

1v1 learning battles.

Example:

```text
Anar vs Ramiz
10 questions
Winner gains Elo and XP
Loser loses small Elo
```

Recommended balance:

```text
Win: +15 Elo
Lose: -3 Elo
```

Losing should not punish too much.

## 5. Inclusive Education System

LogiCora must support children with special learning needs from the beginning.

Use the term:

**Inclusive Education System**

not only "disabled mode".

### 5.1 Individual Learning Path

Every child learns at a different pace.

The system should support personalized learning routes based on:

- Age
- Ability
- Attention span
- Learning speed
- Interests
- Special needs
- Parent/teacher observations
- AI performance analysis

---

### 5.2 Autism Spectrum Support

UX and learning adjustments:

- Calm interface
- Less animation
- Predictable routines
- Structured daily plan
- Low sensory overload
- Simple navigation
- Social skills practice

---

### 5.3 Down Syndrome / Development Delay Support

Adjustments:

- Larger cards
- Simpler tasks
- Visual learning
- Repetition
- Voice explanations
- Slower pacing
- Parent-assisted mode

---

### 5.4 ADHD / Attention Difficulty Support

Adjustments:

- Short tasks
- Focus sessions
- Small rewards
- Minimal distractions
- Chunked lessons
- Frequent feedback

---

### 5.5 Speech Difficulties

Support:

- Pronunciation practice
- AI voice exercises
- Parent reports
- Speech progress tracking
- Simple audio-based tasks

---

### 5.6 Vision / Hearing Limitations

Support:

- High contrast mode
- Screen reader support
- Keyboard navigation
- Subtitles
- Larger fonts
- Future sign language video support

## 6. User Roles

### Student

The central user.

### Parent

Can monitor child progress, courses, attendance, quiz results, warnings, interests, certificates, and reports.

### Teacher

Can create courses, quizzes, groups, live classes, attendance, analytics, and recommendations.

### School Admin

Can see school-level analytics, teacher performance, class activity, attendance, and risk signals.

### Ministry

Optional government layer for anonymized and structured analytics.

### Employer

Can view user-approved portfolio data and request deeper access.

### Institution

Course centers, universities, academies, training providers.

Can create courses, manage groups, issue certificates, run exams/quizzes, and use CRM tools.

## 7. Data Ownership & Privacy

Recommended model:

### Under 18

- Owner: student
- Managed by: parent/guardian

### 18+

- Owner: user

The user controls public visibility.

### Portfolio visibility layers

#### Public Layer

Visible to everyone:

- Name
- Short bio
- Selected skills
- Selected certificates
- Selected projects
- Public achievements

#### Permission Layer

Visible after user approval:

- Deeper skill graph
- Progress trends
- Career AI summary
- Verified learning history

#### Restricted Layer

Visible only to authorized roles:

- Parent
- Teacher
- School
- Institution
- Ministry where applicable

Includes attendance, internal reports, sensitive notes, early warnings, and special needs support data.
